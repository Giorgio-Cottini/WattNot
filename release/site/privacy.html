WattNot privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Summary

WattNot collects no personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no crash reporting.

On your device

WattNot stores the zone you choose and the price files it downloads. These files stay on your iPhone. WattNot deletes price files older than yesterday. Delete the app to remove everything.

Network requests

WattNot downloads public price files from Amazon CloudFront, a content delivery network of Amazon Web Services. A request contains no information about you except what every internet request carries, such as the IP address of your device. CloudFront uses it to deliver the file. Access logging is off, so WattNot keeps no record of these requests. Amazon processes the request under the AWS Privacy Notice.

For zones that are not available yet, a link opens the public price page of Nord Pool in Safari. Nord Pool's own privacy policy applies to that page.

Children

WattNot collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes

A change to this policy appears on this page with a new date. A change that adds any collection of data needs an app update first.
