What the prices are

Day-ahead spot price, excl. VAT, taxes, grid fees and supplier margin. The price on your bill is higher, and your supplier sets it.

WattNot shows each price for one quarter hour, in the subunit of your currency per kWh: cent, öre, øre or grosz. The chart shows the mean of the four quarters of each hour. Touch and hold the chart to read the quarters.

Prices in DKK, NOK, SEK and PLN are converted from EUR/MWh at the European Central Bank reference rate. They show with ≈ and the date of the rate.

When tomorrow's prices arrive

Tomorrow's prices usually arrive after 13:00 Central European Time. Before that, the Tomorrow page says when to expect them. Open the app or pull to refresh after that time.

Zones

WattNot shows prices for these zones:

  • Germany and Luxembourg
  • Denmark West (DK1) and Denmark East (DK2)
  • Norway Southwest (NO2)
  • Sweden Central (SE3) and Sweden South (SE4)
  • Netherlands
  • Poland

Finland, Estonia, Latvia, Norway NO1, NO3, NO4 and NO5, and Sweden SE1 and SE2 are not available yet. No public source licenses their prices for reuse today. For these zones the app links to the public price page of Nord Pool.

Levels

Cheap, normal, expensive and very expensive compare each price with the previous 7 days of the zone. A price below the 25th percentile is cheap, and a price above the 75th percentile is expensive. A price above the 75th percentile plus 1.5 times the distance between the 25th and the 75th percentile is very expensive. "Zero or negative" marks a price at or below zero.
